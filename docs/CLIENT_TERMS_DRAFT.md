# Proposed client terms — private draft, not approved for publication

Owner and qualified legal review required before setting TERMS_APPROVED. This is a working draft, not legal advice or a published contract. Resolve the open decisions below and update the website, checkout acceptance, confirmation emails, and TERMS_VERSION together.

## Service description

Blazing Visuals, publicly known as Blazevisionz, provides individual portrait photography in Spring/Houston, Texas. Essential: $100 total, 30 minutes, one person, one outfit, one location, 3 edited JPEGs. Signature: $150 total, 60 minutes, one person, up to two outfits, one location, 5 edited JPEGs. Both include creative and posing direction, private proofs, and a complimentary ten-minute phone consultation after verified deposit and intake.

Additional edited images: $20 each. RAW files are excluded. Studio rental, special locations, and extended travel require a separate client-approved quote. Introductory prices cover the first twenty completed paid sessions; later changes require an owner decision and apply only to new agreed bookings.

## Payment proposal

Essential deposit $30 / balance $70. Signature deposit $45 / balance $105. Deposit credited toward total. Proposed balance deadline: end of the shoot. Final downloads require full verified payment. No subscriptions, recurring charges, or automatic future card charges.

Owner decisions: tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive pricing and applicable tax handling; refund/credit treatment; overdue balances; additional costs. Do not describe a deposit as nonrefundable until reviewed and approved.

## Booking and changes

Only listed appointments are available. Confirmation requires verified deposit; completing a redirect is not confirmation. Time zone: America/Chicago. Consultation follows paid deposit and intake and is by phone for a maximum of ten minutes.

Owner decisions: required cancellation and rescheduling notice; late arrival/no-show policy; number of changes allowed; weather, illness, photographer cancellation, and emergencies; studio/location refund treatment; refund timing. Do not publish placeholder deadlines or penalties as an active policy.

## Proofs and delivery

Clients select their included images from private proofs. Final format: edited JPEG. Proposed target from the existing brand guide is three business days after selection, but owner must confirm capacity and publish a realistic deadline. Define proof turnaround, gallery expiration, backup period, revision scope, and delays before launch.

## Rights, consent, privacy

Legal review required for copyright ownership, personal/commercial usage rights, releases for minors/third parties, liability, indemnity, disputes, and location permissions. Portfolio permission is optional and separate from purchase. No private intake or gallery is published.

Set an actual privacy contact, retention/deletion schedule, access rules, external gallery/email provider disclosures, and consent withdrawal process. The client portal uses confidential bearer links; clients must keep them private. Do not claim specific retention or deletion timelines until implemented.

## Required before publication

Actual business identity and public contact information; approved payment/tax treatment; cancellation/refund rules; delivery deadlines; rights/releases; privacy terms; legal review of enforceable clauses. Publish a versioned policy and save the version and acceptance time with each booking.
