## Proposed cancellation and rescheduling — approve or revise

Clients contact the studio to request changes. A replacement date requires written studio confirmation and availability. The deposit carries to an approved replacement date. Rescheduling is handled reasonably: any fee must be explained and agreed before the change. Weather, illness, and emergencies are considered individually. No automatic penalty or undisclosed charge applies.

If the client cancels before photography or editing begins, refund payments for work not performed, less only nonrecoverable third-party expenses disclosed and approved before they were incurred. Itemize any performed work and previously agreed expenses before proposing a deduction. Deposits are not automatically nonrefundable, and this draft does not invent a no-show penalty.

If the studio cancels and no replacement is mutually accepted, refund amounts paid for services not provided. Weather, illness, and unsafe conditions follow the same agreed replacement or unused-service refund approach. Refunds return through the original payment channel; bank processing time is not guaranteed.

Late arrival may reduce the available session time because later commitments remain protected. Extra time or costs require agreement. No arbitrary notice deadline or penalty is set by this draft.

## Proposed privacy and usage — approve or revise

Booking and intake details arrange and deliver the service. Stripe processes payments; Cloudflare hosts booking records and encrypted access tokens; Resend sends verification and transactional messages. Clients enter card numbers on Stripe Checkout, not this website. Identify the gallery provider before sharing a gallery.

Private portal links grant access to one booking and must not be shared. Portfolio permission is optional and separate from purchase. Clients may contact the studio about access, corrections, deletion requests, or withdrawal of portfolio permission. Explain records retained for payment, tax, or dispute obligations; do not promise an automatic deletion period before implementing it.

Image-use permissions, copyright/license wording, minors' consent, location permissions, and releases must match the written agreement. A RAW add-on does not itself transfer copyright. No blanket liability waiver or other unreviewed legal clause is added.
