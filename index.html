06 / GOOD TO KNOW

A LITTLE
CLARITY.

How does the consultation work?

After your deposit is verified, complete your creative intake and choose an open phone consultation time. The call lasts up to 10 minutes and covers your aesthetic, outfits, posing, and location.

What do I receive?

Essential includes 3 edited JPEGs; Signature includes 5. You select your favorites from a private proof gallery. Additional edited images cost $20 each. RAW files are not included.

Are studio and travel costs included?

Studio rental, special locations, and extended travel require a separate quote that you approve before booking.

Can I book any Wednesday through Sunday?

Only released appointments are available. The calendar accounts for rotating work shifts, family commitments, preparation, travel, and protected creative work.

Will my photographs be shared publicly?

Portfolio permission is optional and recorded separately from your booking. Your private gallery and intake are not published.
