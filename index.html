08 / GOOD TO KNOW

A LITTLE
CLARITY.

Can commercial or event clients book directly?

No. Fashion and editorial, local business, and event inquiries require consultation first. Quote, agreement, retainer, and confirmed booking happen only after scope approval.

How long are consultations?

Fashion and editorial inquiries require a complimentary 20-minute consultation. Local business inquiries require 15 minutes. Events require 15-20 minutes. Personal sessions keep the 10-minute phone consultation after deposit and intake.

What do personal sessions receive?

Essential includes 3 edited JPEGs; Signature includes 5. You select your favorites from a private proof gallery. Additional edited images cost $20 each. RAW files are not included.

Are studio and travel costs included?

Studio rental, special locations, extended travel, location expenses, and commercial licensing require separate scope approval before booking.

Will my photographs be shared publicly?

Portfolio permission is optional and recorded separately from your booking. Your private gallery and intake are not published.
